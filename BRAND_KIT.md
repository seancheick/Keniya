# Keniya — Brand Kit (superseded)

The October 2026 revamp replaced the original cream / Fraunces / terracotta look with the
"Wrapped & Sealed" world. **DESIGN.md is now the single source of truth** for colors, type,
components and motion, and `src/app/globals.css` holds the tokens in code. Product and copy rules
live in PRODUCT.md.

What carries over unchanged:

- **Name:** Keniya, from *kɛnɛya*, "health" in Dioula. Use *kɛnɛya* (lowercase, italic) only in the
  origin story, never as the wordmark.
- **Wordmark:** "Keniya." with the period as the brand mark, now coral (`coral-deep` #B23A2C on
  cream, `coral` #FF796C on charcoal).
- **Voice:** plain, warm, direct; short sentences; no medical claims.
