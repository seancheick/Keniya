### How each rule is classified

| Tier | What it covers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Verified label data** | What the exact package in hand declares | Nutrition Facts, ingredients, Contains/may-contain statement, single serving, barcode |
| **2. Evidence-based safety exclusions** | Food-safety risks and clinically relevant restrictions | Pregnancy no-go foods (raw/unpasteurized, deli and cured meat, high-mercury fish, alcohol, liver); caffeine limits in the pregnancy boxes; a customer's stated allergens |
| **3. Keniya selection policy** | Our own standards, not clinical guidelines | Carb, added-sugar, sodium and saturated-fat limits per box; treats up to 5 g added sugar; no synthetic dyes; at most 2 of a type; at most 4 chocolate snacks; protein/fiber minimums; pregnancy-style food safety in Postpartum |
| **4. Reviewer approval** | Your sign-off | Each product, and each box |
