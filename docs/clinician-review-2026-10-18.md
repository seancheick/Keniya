| Traditional Medicinals: exact product must match | **Every box now requires the ingredient list on file.** Traditional Medicinals is out until we have its label, UPC and photo. |
| Some nutrition numbers look calculated | **The system now spots numbers a printed FDA label can't show** (like 99 cal or 12.9 g). A product can't be package-verified until the printed values are entered. These are marked in section 3. |
| 2,300 mg box sodium total isn't meaningful | **Dropped.** |
| Chocolate caffeine estimate | **Estimates stay internal screening only and are never printed for customers.** The website now says caffeine is "checked", not "known". |
| Postpartum isn't a breastfeeding requirement | **The website now says** the pregnancy-style food-safety screen is Keniya's conservative choice, not a breastfeeding requirement. |
| GDM: 15 g isn't a prescription | **The website now says** the 15 g limit is how Keniya selects snacks, not a medical target or a prescription. Owner decision: your approval covers the GDM box. |
| Allergies | **We exclude only the allergens a customer tells us about.** For that customer, both "contains" and "may contain" exclude a product, and so does any label we can't verify. We never promise allergen-free. |

### How each rule is classified

| Tier | What it covers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Verified label data** | What the exact package in hand declares | Nutrition Facts, ingredients, Contains/may-contain statement, single serving, barcode |
| **2. Evidence-based safety exclusions** | Food-safety risks and clinically relevant restrictions | Pregnancy no-go foods (raw/unpasteurized, deli and cured meat, high-mercury fish, alcohol, liver); caffeine limits in the pregnancy boxes; a customer's stated allergens |
| **3. Keniya selection policy** | Our own standards, not clinical guidelines | Carb, added-sugar, sodium and saturated-fat limits per box; treats up to 5 g added sugar; no synthetic dyes; at most 2 of a type; at most 4 chocolate snacks; protein/fiber minimums; pregnancy-style food safety in Postpartum |
| **4. Reviewer approval** | Your sign-off | Each product, and each box |
