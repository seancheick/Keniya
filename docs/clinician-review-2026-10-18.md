## What we need from you

We have built the first version of our six snack boxes (14 snacks each, 22 different products in total). Before we buy anything in bulk, we need your clinical OK.

Please do three things:

1. **Answer the 7 questions** in section 1. Each one has our proposed answer, so a simple "agree" or "disagree" is enough.
2. **Approve or decline each product** in section 2. Each product lists its ingredients and nutrition.
3. **Approve each box** in section 3, or tell us what to change.

You can write directly in this file, or reply with numbers ("Q1 agree, product 5 not approved because…").

**Please note:** our software screened every product from its label (ingredients, nutrition, allergens) against the rules below. Nothing here has had a clinical review yet. That is what we are asking you for. If you decline a product, we take it out and replace it.

### The six boxes, in plain words

| Box | Who it's for | Main rules per snack |
|---|---|---|
| **Pregnancy** | Pregnant women | No pregnancy no-go foods (raw/unpasteurized, deli/cured meat, high-mercury fish, alcohol, risky herbs). Caffeine ≤50 mg per snack and ≤100 mg per box. At most 4 chocolate snacks. |
| **Gestational Diabetes** | Pregnant women with GDM | All Pregnancy rules, plus ≤15 g total carbs and ≤3 g added sugar (treats up to 5 g, at most 2 treats). |
| **Postpartum** | New and nursing mothers | Same food-safety rules as Pregnancy. Caffeine ≤100 mg per snack and ≤150 mg per box. More protein and hydration. |
| **Blood Sugar** | Adults managing blood sugar | ≤15 g total carbs and ≤3 g added sugar (treats up to 5 g, at most 3 treats). Each snack needs protein, fiber or a whole-food base. |
| **Heart** | Heart-healthy eating | ≤140 mg sodium, ≤2 g saturated fat (≤4 g for plain nuts and seeds), ≤5 g added sugar, at least 4 nut/seed snacks. |
| **GLP-1** | People on GLP-1 medicines | ≤20 g total carbs and ≤5 g added sugar. Protein- or fiber-forward, small portions. Drinks must be unsweetened. |

Every box: no synthetic dyes, single-serve sealed packs only. A customer's allergy answers are a hard rule at packing; we never promise allergen-free.

- *Our proposal:* pasteurized or roasted nuts and seeds pass the pregnancy "cooked or pasteurized" check. Anything sold as "raw" or "sprouted" gets a case-by-case review.
