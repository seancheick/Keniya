# Store scan checklist

Send one photo set per product. With all of it, a product can be screened without a second trip. Without it, the product stays on hold.

1. **Front of the pack.** Name, flavor and net weight.
2. **The full Nutrition Facts panel.** Include the serving size and servings per container at the top. Cropping off either is the most common reason a product can't be scored.
3. **Ingredients, plus the "Contains" / "Made in a facility…" statement.** The ingredients do most of the work now:
   - caffeine is filled in from them;
   - dyes, sugar alcohols, alcohol and watchlist herbs are caught automatically.
   - Get the whole list, including small print in parentheses.
4. **Single-serve.**
   - If the item comes as a multipack, photograph the outer box showing the count (for example "12 × 1 oz bars").
   - Also photograph one individual wrapper. Each pick must be one sealed serving.
5. **Best-by date and lot code.** Packed stock needs at least 90 days left.
6. **Barcode.** On the single pack. If the outer box has a different barcode, photograph both and say which is which.
7. **Price sticker** (or the shelf tag).
8. **Caffeine, only if printed.** This applies to coffee, tea, matcha or guarana items: they're the only ones the system can't estimate. Look for a "caffeine" line on the pack, or note the brand's website value.

## Quick reject on the shelf (skip the photos)

- Synthetic dyes: Red 40, Yellow 5/6, Blue 1/2, "FD&C …", "artificial color". This applies to every box.
- Alcohol as an ingredient (rum, liqueur, wine). "Rum flavor" and vanilla extract are fine.
- Raw / unpasteurized milk or cheese, raw sprouts, liver or pâté.

## Usually fine now (no longer a hold)

- **Chocolate or cocoa.** Caffeine is estimated from the pack weight, and a small bar is far under the cap.
  - Up to 4 chocolate picks per pregnancy-family box.
- **Stevia, monk fruit, allulose.** These are information for the customer, not a block.
- **For Blood Sugar / Gestational Diabetes:** a small portioned treat with up to 5 g added sugar.
  - Total carbs still ≤15 g.
  - At most 3 treats per Blood Sugar box, 2 per GDM box.
